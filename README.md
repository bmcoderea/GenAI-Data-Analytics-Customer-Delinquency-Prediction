# GenAI Data Analytics – Customer Delinquency Prediction

## Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the GenAI Powered Data Analytics Job Simulation on Forage. The objective was to analyze customer delinquency data, identify high-risk customer segments, and recommend AI-driven strategies to improve collections performance.

## Business Problem

Financial institutions face challenges in identifying customers who are likely to become delinquent. Early identification enables targeted interventions, reduced collection costs, and improved customer outcomes.

## Tasks Completed

### 1. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

* Analyzed customer demographic and financial attributes.
* Identified missing values, outliers, and data quality issues.
* Explored relationships between customer characteristics and delinquency.

### 2. Risk Profiling

* Identified customer segments with elevated delinquency risk.
* Examined behavioral and financial indicators associated with missed payments.

### 3. Predictive Analytics Framework

* Defined key predictors of delinquency.
* Proposed an AI-based approach for customer risk scoring and prioritization.

### 4. Business Reporting & Storytelling

* Converted analytical findings into business insights.
* Presented recommendations for improving collections effectiveness.

### 5. AI-Driven Collections Strategy

* Developed a roadmap for implementing predictive risk monitoring.
* Suggested customer-specific intervention strategies based on risk levels.
